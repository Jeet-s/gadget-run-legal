Privacy Policy

Kiko Gadget Run · Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains what information Kiko Gadget Run ("the game") handles. The game is published by JeeTech ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect, store or receive any personal information ourselves.
  • Your progress (scores, coins, upgrades, missions and settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are requested as child-directed and non-personalised.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, coins balance, gadget upgrades, missions, daily-gift streak and settings in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show rewarded video ads (which you choose to watch for an in-game reward) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. We configure every ad request as follows:

  • Child-directed treatment is turned on (in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA).
  • Ads are non-personalised: they are not based on your interests or past behaviour.
  • The ad content rating is limited to "G" (suitable for general audiences).
  • On Android, the app does not access the Google advertising ID. On iOS, the app does not ask for permission to track you.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as your IP address (used to estimate a general location), device type, operating system, app version and how you interact with ads. Google processes this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the game shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Ad privacy choices.

Children

The game is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game has no features for children to share personal information. Ads are child-directed and non-personalised as described above. If you are a parent and have questions, contact us at the address below.

Your choices

  • You can reset the advertising settings on your device at any time.
  • You can delete all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
